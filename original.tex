Overall, projected gradient descent (orange) enforces the constraint directly and is the fastest method for achieving feasibility. Penalty methods (green, red, purple) provide a softer approach, where feasibility depends on the choice of \(\lambda\). Larger values of \(\lambda\) improve constraint satisfaction but can affect optimisation behaviour. Unconstrained gradient descent (blue) is not suitable for constrained problems, since it does not account for feasibility and may remain outside the admissible set.
